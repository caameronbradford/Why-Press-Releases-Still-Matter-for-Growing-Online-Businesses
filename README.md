# Why Press Releases Still Matter for Growing Online Businesses

Online businesses have more ways to communicate than ever before. A company can publish on social media, send newsletters, update its website, create videos, or speak directly to customers through online communities. With so many channels available, the traditional press release might appear less important than it once was.

Yet press releases continue to serve a specific purpose. They provide businesses with a structured way to document important developments and communicate factual information to journalists, industry publications, customers, partners, and other interested audiences.

For growing online businesses, this structured communication can become particularly useful as the company begins producing news beyond routine marketing.

## Creating a Clear Record of Company News

Growing companies change quickly. They introduce products, enter new markets, hire executives, form partnerships, publish research, and sometimes raise funding.

Social posts can announce these developments, but they are usually brief and quickly replaced by newer content.

A press release creates a more formal record.

It can clearly state what happened, when it happened, who was involved, and why the development matters. Someone researching the company months later can use these announcements to understand how the business has developed.

This historical record becomes increasingly useful as a company grows.

## Separating News From Everyday Marketing

Online businesses publish marketing content constantly.

Product descriptions, advertisements, promotional emails, landing pages, and social posts are usually designed to persuade potential customers.

Press releases serve a different function.

A useful release should focus on facts and news value rather than simply describing a product positively. It should answer basic questions about an announcement and provide enough context for an outside reader to understand it.

This distinction matters because not every company update deserves a press release.

A minor website redesign might belong on social media. A major acquisition, expansion, funding round, original research project, or significant product launch may justify a more formal announcement.

## Giving Journalists Organized Information

Journalists often work under tight deadlines. When they investigate a company announcement, they need information that can be understood quickly.

A properly structured release places essential facts near the beginning.

The opening should generally explain:

- Who is making the announcement
- What has happened
- When it happened
- Where it is relevant
- Why the development matters

Supporting information can then provide additional context, data, quotes, and company background.

The Public Relations Society of America recommends an inverted-pyramid approach in which the most important facts appear first. This structure allows readers to understand the core announcement without reading every paragraph.

## Supporting Product Launches

Product launches are common news events for online companies, particularly SaaS and ecommerce businesses.

A launch announcement can explain what the product does, who it is designed for, when it becomes available, and what has changed compared with previous offerings.

The important part is keeping the announcement factual.

Statements such as "the greatest solution ever created" provide little useful information. Specific details about functionality, availability, integrations, pricing changes, or intended users are much easier to evaluate.

The same principle applies to software updates. A substantial new feature may deserve an announcement when it meaningfully changes what customers can do.

## Distribution Needs to Match the Audience

Writing a press release does not automatically mean that relevant people will see it. Distribution is a separate decision.

A specialized SaaS company may need technology and business publications, while an ecommerce company could have a different media audience. Geographic relevance may also matter when an announcement concerns a particular part of the United States.

Businesses can use direct journalist outreach, company newsrooms, distribution networks, or combinations of these approaches.

Services are also becoming more specific about the audiences they address. <a href="http://us-newswire.com">US newswire</a> focuses on distributing announcements from U.S. businesses, ecommerce brands, and SaaS companies through identified publication options while providing reporting on confirmed placements.

Regardless of the distribution method, relevance remains more important than simply sending an announcement to as many destinations as possible.

## Announcements Can Support Media Research

Press releases also make information easier to find when journalists research companies.

Imagine a reporter researching how a software company has developed over several years. Previous announcements about funding, leadership changes, acquisitions, and major products can provide useful dates and background.

The reporter should still independently verify important facts, but organized company announcements provide a starting point.

This is one reason businesses often maintain online newsrooms or press sections.

PRSA has noted that an online newsroom can give journalists access not only to releases but also to background information, photographs, videos, executive biographies, and media contact details.

## Press Releases Can Explain Partnerships

Partnership announcements are common among growing digital companies.

However, simply announcing that two organizations are "excited to partner" provides limited information.

A stronger release explains what the partnership actually changes.

Will two software products integrate? Will customers gain access to a new service? Are the companies entering a new market together?

These details turn a corporate statement into understandable news.

Quotes can provide additional context, but they should contribute information rather than repeat the headline using enthusiastic language.

## Smaller Businesses Can Communicate Professionally

Formal public communication is not limited to large corporations.

A small ecommerce business might announce a warehouse expansion. A young SaaS company might release original industry research. A startup might appoint an experienced executive.

A clear release gives each organization a consistent format for presenting such information.

Professional communication does not require complicated vocabulary. In fact, simple writing is often more effective.

Short paragraphs, specific facts, useful numbers, descriptive headlines, and accessible contact information make announcements easier to understand.

## Press Releases Work Alongside Other Channels

Press releases should not replace every other communication channel.

Social media is useful for immediate conversation. Email can reach existing customers directly. Blog posts allow companies to explain subjects in greater depth. A newsroom can maintain an archive of official information.

Press releases occupy another position within this system.

One announcement can become a source for several communication activities. The company might publish the release in its newsroom, share the news through social channels, mention it in a newsletter, and provide the information to relevant journalists.

The channels support each other rather than competing for the same purpose.

## Newsworthiness Still Comes First

The existence of distribution technology does not make every announcement interesting.

Before writing a release, businesses should ask whether something has actually changed.

Does the announcement affect customers? Does it introduce meaningful data? Does it represent an important company milestone? Is there a broader industry connection?

Guidance from the <a href="https://www.prsa.org/">Public Relations Society of America</a> emphasizes the importance of relevant, factual communication and understanding what journalists and their audiences actually need.

That distinction becomes especially important as online businesses grow and generate more internal activity.

## A Continuing Role in Digital Business Communication

The internet has changed how company news travels, but it has not eliminated the need for structured announcements.

For growing online businesses, press releases provide a practical format for recording meaningful developments, organizing facts, supporting media outreach, and maintaining an accessible history of company activity.

Their value depends on how they are used.

A release without genuine news may simply add noise. A clear announcement built around a relevant development can provide journalists and other readers with useful information in a familiar structure.

That is why press releases continue to have a place alongside newer communication channels. Their role is not to replace social media, blogs, newsletters, or direct outreach. It is to give significant business news a clear, organized format that can be referenced, distributed, and understood.
